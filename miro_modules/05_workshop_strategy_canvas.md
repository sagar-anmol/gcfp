## Interactive Workshop Prompts for Miro AI:

Copy and paste these exact prompts into the **Miro AI Assistant** on your board:

### Prompt 1: For "Map a process"
> "Map an end-to-end service blueprint for a hybrid eldercare platform. Step 1: Senior citizen wakes up at 3:00 AM and talks to a 24/7 empathetic AI voice agent in Hindi/Punjabi. Step 2: AI logs health telemetry and tags interests. Step 3: AI matches compatible peers within 3km for a Saturday high-tea kirtan/storytelling meetup. Step 4: Human Relationship Manager reviews proposal in 10 seconds and approves. Step 5: Gig driver carpools seniors to venue. Step 6: Automated weekly video digest sent to child living abroad in Canada."

### Prompt 2: For "Map user journey"
> "Create a two-track customer journey map comparing: (1) A lonely 74-year-old retired Army Colonel in Chandigarh living alone in a 1-Kanal villa with 3:00 AM insomnia, moving from skepticism to pride as a member of an exclusive storytelling club. (2) His 42-year-old NRI daughter living in Toronto, moving from guilt and distance anxiety to complete peace of mind through automated Sunday WhatsApp video digests."

### Prompt 3: For "Build a kanban"
> "Build an operational triage kanban board with columns: Inbound AI Triage Alert, RM Home Visit Required, Dynamic Gig Dispatched, Club Gathering Active, and Telemetry Delivered to Family. Populate cards with eldercare tasks in Chandigarh sectors."

### Prompt 4: For "Build a roadmap"
> "Build a 4-quarter strategic product and expansion roadmap for an autonomous senior living platform. Q1: 15-Family Tricity Alpha (WhatsApp Indic Voice AI). Q2: B2B Hospital Sponsorships (Fortis/Max Mohali free halls). Q3: Expat Marketing in Canada/UK ($99/mo). Q4: Expansion to Ludhiana & Jalandhar."
