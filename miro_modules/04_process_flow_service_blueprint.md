## Swimlane Process Map Steps (For Miro Swimlanes):

### Lane 1: Senior Citizen (User Actions)
1. Wakes up at 3:00 AM with anxiety / joint pain.
2. Talks to AI phone buddy in Punjabi/Hindi; shares 1971 war memories.
3. Receives automated WhatsApp invite on Thursday for Saturday Tea & Storytelling.
4. Boards carpool cab at 3:45 PM with two neighborhood peers.
5. Enjoys Saturday High-Tea at partner cafe; delivers 10-min talk; plays chess.
6. Returns home energized and dignified.

### Lane 2: AI Voice Engine (Front-End & Backend)
1. Answers phone call within 2 rings with natural Indian voice cadence.
2. Recalls past conversation context (*"Uncle, did your knee pain subside?"*).
3. Evaluates speech acoustic biomarkers (tremor, pauses, depressive words).
4. Matches senior vector embeddings with peers in adjacent sectors.
5. Computes carpool route and generates 10-second Proposal Card for RM.

### Lane 3: Relationship Manager (Human Touchpoint)
1. Monitors morning mood summary dashboard.
2. Conducts bi-weekly 45-minute home visit; drinks tea, verifies safety and medicines.
3. Reviews AI Event Proposal Card on mobile app; verifies physical mobility notes; taps "Approve".
4. Hosts Saturday High-Tea session; takes photos and short videos.
5. Submits weekly progress notes on portal.

### Lane 4: Dynamic Gig & Medical Partners (Execution)
1. Gig Driver receives optimized carpool pickup route for 3 seniors.
2. Safely escorts seniors from door to car and into venue.
3. Partner Phlebotomist (Dr. Lal) visits for morning fasting blood draw.
4. Gig Companion accompanies senior to hospital OPD and handles queue waiting.

### Lane 5: NRI Expat Child (Customer & Stakeholder)
1. Subscribes online from Toronto/London/Bay Area ($59–$129/mo).
2. Sets emergency escalation contacts and parent preferences.
3. Receives weekly Sunday WhatsApp Video Digest and health dashboard.
4. Auto-renews subscription with high loyalty (24+ months retention).
